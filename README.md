TechWomenAZ is a community committed to helping women thrive as leaders and innovators in technology through career development, mentorship, learning opportunities, and meaningful networking connections(https://techwomenaz.org/). 

A problem TechWomenAZ, a non-profit organization, faces is trouble working through its mentor/mentee System.

As students of the community, we are trying to solve the problem of assigning the right mentors to mentees; we created an app where the mentor or mentee chats with an AI agent. The AI Agent asks questions to the user based on the context of the user's previous responses to help generate new, unique questions to help evaluate the goals the client is trying to achieve through the TechWomenAZ program. Overall, it will significantly help the individual pair up with a Mentor or a mentee and also make the process way easier.

The AI generates and quantifies values based on the response given and accurately evaluates and matches you to a mentee/mentor.
